Like a heartfelt letter wrapped in petals, To You Floral Shop is a brand built around emotion, warmth, and quiet sincerity. Inspired by the K-pop boy group SEVENTEEN’s song “To You,” the brand reflects themes of heartfelt connection, just like the song itself. A delicate floral detail was incorporated into the logo to clearly represent the floral shop while keeping the design elegant and refined. This element was designed to feel soft and intentional, adding a romantic touch without overpowering the overall composition. A combination of script and serif typography was used to balance emotion and structure. The script font brings warmth and a handwritten, heartfelt feel, while the serif font adds sophistication and a sense of luxury.
