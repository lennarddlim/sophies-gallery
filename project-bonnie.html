Bonnie Brooms is a playful and vibrant brand designed to make cleaning feel fun and approachable. The name’s alliteration and lively sound bring personality and energy, while the visual identity embraces bold, unexpected colors like orange and purple to break away from traditional cleaning palettes. With a hint of wackiness and a focus on creativity, the brand encourages a fresh, engaging perspective on everyday cleaning tasks. Its design is meant to stand out and spark a smile while still feeling practical and relatable.
