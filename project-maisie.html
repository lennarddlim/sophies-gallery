Maisie & Belle is a refined and adaptable brand created with future growth in mind. The visual identity is intentionally clean and uncluttered, allowing the brand to expand beyond cleaning into services like gardening and home care. A strong, elegant wordmark is paired with an abstract house form, subtly suggesting greenery or trees to reflect a broader lifestyle offering. The overall design remains simple yet flexible, ensuring the brand feels timeless, approachable, and ready to evolve as the business grows.
